# Screenshot Gallery

Browse the screenshots RuneLite already takes for you — level-ups, pets, collection log slots, deaths, clue rewards,
boss kills and more — right in the sidebar, instead of digging through folders.

- **Each picture edge to edge**, newest first with a heading per month, its title and date on it and a badge for its
  type (the skill's icon for a level-up).
- **Albums that fill themselves**: one per type you have (Boss kills, Deaths, Levels, Pets, Clues...), each with its
  count, added the first time you take one.
- **Your own albums** too: make one, drag a picture onto it, right-click to rename or delete it. A picture can be in
  several albums.
- **Click a picture** to see it big. Previous / Next or the arrow keys step through; Delete removes it from the gallery.
- **On this day.** The top of the panel shows a screenshot you took on today's date in an earlier year, and when you
  log in a chat line tells you about it (once a day; turn it off in the settings).
- **Three sizes**: Big, Medium or Small. Search by name, or pick one character.

The screenshots come from RuneLite's own **Screenshot** plugin: turn it on and tick what it should keep. Each new one
is copied into the gallery (as a smaller JPEG) the moment it's taken. Older ones: click **Import**, then Open (on
Windows, pick your `.runelite` folder first). The gallery only works with its own copies — your original screenshots
are never changed or deleted — and it sends nothing anywhere.
